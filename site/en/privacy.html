Note Privacy

Last updated September 23, 2026.

Verso has no accounts and collects no usage statistics. It only goes online to fetch the lyrics of the song that's playing and, if you ask it to, to translate them.

What leaves your computer

  • LRCLIB: title, artist, album and duration of the track playing on Spotify or YouTube Music, to fetch synced lyrics. It's an open, community-maintained database.
  • Translation: only if you turn on translation in Verso's menu. The lyric lines go to Verso's translation server (hosted on Cloudflare), which forwards them to Microsoft Translator. The translation is kept in a shared cache with nothing that identifies who asked for it. Your IP address is used only to limit abuse, as a hash, and that counter is deleted within two days.

Nothing else is sent: no identifiers for your computer and no data from your Spotify or YouTube account.

What stays on your computer

  • The app's preferences (position, appearance, shortcuts), in a local config file.
  • Translations already made, cached so the same lyrics aren't requested twice.

Uninstalling the app and deleting those folders removes everything.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Fontshare and Google Fonts. To link to the latest version, it queries the public GitHub API. We use no cookies and no analytics.
