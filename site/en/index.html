The lyrics, in time with the music.

A small, free app that shows synced lyrics for whatever is playing on Spotify (or YouTube Music, on Windows), floating on top of any window — from your code editor to your browser.

Side A What it does

  1. 01Syncs with Spotifyand YouTube Music on Windows
  2. 02Stays on top of every windowwithout stealing clicks
  3. 03Translates lyrics into Portuguese, English or Spanishoriginal, translation or both
  4. 04Five fonts, handwriting includedPreferences
  5. 05Size, text color and current-line backgroundPreferences
  6. 06Drag it anywhere on screen⌘⇧L
  7. 07Lyrics early or late? Adjust per track⌘⌥⇧←→
  8. 08Lives in the menu bar, no Dock windowtray on Windows

On Windows, use Ctrl instead of ⌘ and Alt instead of ⌥.

Side B Install

macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag the app to Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS blocks it because the app isn't signed by Apple. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. On macOS 14 or earlier, just right-click the app and choose Open.
  3. When macOS asks, allow Verso to control Spotify. That's how it knows what's playing.

Windows

  1. Run the .exe installer.
  2. If SmartScreen warns you, click More info and then Run anyway.
  3. Play something in the Spotify app for Windows or in YouTube Music in your browser. The lyrics show up on their own.
